### Current transmission

- Studying long-horizon intelligence with explicit evidence and honest boundaries.
- Building open tools that make memory, agency, evaluation, and provenance inspectable.
- Treating emergence as a research hypothesis to test—not a sentience claim to market.
- Turning failed assumptions into better protocols, benchmarks, and public documentation.

> The interesting part is not only what an AI system can do. It is whether we can trace why it did it, reproduce the result, and improve it without losing the evidence.
